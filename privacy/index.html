IPolyTv Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

IPolyTv is a local media player for IPTV sources supplied by the user. It is free for 7 days; afterwards a one-time lifetime license is required. It does not provide channels, streams, playlists, media content, user accounts, advertising, analytics, or a remote IPolyTv server.

Data used by the app

  • M3U/M3U8 and XMLTV source addresses, the Xtream server address, and Xtream credentials are stored only on the user's device. These source addresses and credentials are kept in secure storage.
  • A copy of an imported M3U/M3U8 file is stored in the app's private persistent file area for reopening and refreshing the source. It may contain stream links and provider-specified HTTP headers. The app's copy is deleted when the source is removed or replaced; the original file is not modified. iOS device backups may include this persistent file.
  • Program reminders are scheduled only by the device operating system. No remote notification server or push token is used. Notifications display generic text on the lock screen; channel identifiers, program times, and sanitized titles are kept on the device. App reminders are removed when the source is replaced or deleted. Android may reschedule saved reminders after a device restart.
  • Favorites, viewing history, appearance and language preferences, EPG mappings, and category settings are stored only on the device.
  • For fast startup and offline browsing, the last successfully loaded channel list is kept in the app's private cache directory. This cache stays within the app's storage and is excluded from iOS and Android backups. Xtream usernames and passwords are not written to this cache; M3U stream links are stored as supplied by the provider. Offline browsing does not make streams available offline.
  • TV guide (EPG) data is downloaded from the Xtream provider, the address in the playlist header, or the user's XMLTV address. It is kept only in the app cache and removed when the source is deleted.
  • To manage the trial and license, the trial start date, the last time the app was seen running, and whether the license is unlocked are kept in the device's secure storage. This is never sent to IPolyTv or any other server.
  • The license is purchased only through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store processes your payment details; IPolyTv never sees or stores card or payment information. Whether the license was bought on your store account is verified with the store's own records.
  • IPolyTv does not collect this data, send it to its own server, sell it, share it, or use it for advertising or analytics.

Your IPTV provider

IPolyTv supports HTTP and HTTPS source addresses. HTTP connections are unencrypted; the connection type depends on the source offered by your provider.

When you add a source, your device connects directly to the IPTV or EPG provider you choose. That provider may process your IP address, connection times, and request details under its own privacy policy. IPolyTv is not responsible for those providers' data processing.

Retention and deletion

Deleting your IPTV source in the app removes its secure-storage records and channel-list and EPG caches. Replacing the source also removes the old cache. Android automatic app backup is disabled for this app. On iOS, the device may retain keychain data if the app is reinstalled with the same bundle identifier; deleting the source from inside the app clears that record.

Content responsibility

Only add lawful sources you are authorized to access. IPolyTv does not provide, host, sell, distribute, or recommend content.
