IPolyTv Support

For questions, purchase or license issues, or bug reports, email support@ipolytv.com.

Restoring your purchase

If you bought the lifetime license before, open IPolyTv, go to the license screen and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with.

Playback problems

IPolyTv only plays the IPTV source you add yourself. Channel availability, stream quality and connection limits are decided by your provider. If a stream does not play, check your source with your provider first.

About content

IPolyTv does not provide, host, sell, distribute or recommend channels, playlists, movies, series or IPTV services. You are responsible for using only sources you are authorized to access.
